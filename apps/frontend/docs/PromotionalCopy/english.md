#### **So, what is BioPal?**

BioPal is a learning platform designed specifically for _Campbell Biology_. It transforms the traditional experience of reading a textbook into a dynamic, collaborative, and personalized journey of discovery.

- **📖 Read, but not alone**
  While you read the textbook, BioPal opens a "Companion Panel" right beside it. Based on the page you're on, it will:
  - **Display a Knowledge Graph**: Connect abstract concepts with a visual map, helping you build a systematic framework of knowledge.
  - **Offer Contextual Quizzes**: Instantly test your understanding of the material and get detailed feedback.
  - **Act as an AI Tutor**: Always ready to answer your questions and dive deeper into any concept you're curious about.

- **📊 Review, but with insight**
  Every learning action you take is recorded to create your personal "Learning Dashboard."
  - **Track your progress with data**: See your learning trajectory and accuracy trends clearly through intuitive charts.
  - **Pinpoint your weak spots**: The system automatically identifies the topics you struggle with the most, making your review sessions far more effective.

- **🧠 Memorize, but without the struggle**
  We've built in a smart review system based on the principles of spaced repetition.
  - **Plan your reviews scientifically**: Automatically schedules the most effective time for you to review material, fighting the forgetting curve.
  - **Organize your mistakes automatically**: All your incorrect answers go into a dedicated "Wrong Answer Notebook," making it easy to tackle them in one place.

- **💬 Discuss, without limits**
  Learning shouldn't be a solo battle.
  - **Start contextual conversations**: You can discuss any page or question, sharing ideas and sparking insights with biology enthusiasts from around the world.
